# Pantry & Table — Monthly Meal & Checkers Shopping Guide

**Pantry & Table** is a practical monthly meal planner and in-store shopping companion built around a single **~R1,476** grocery plan for **Checkers The Grove Mall** (Willow Park Manor, Pretoria East). It helps you stretch everyday South African pantry staples into a full month of hearty, home-cooked dinners—without daily guesswork or food waste.

---

## What You Can Do on the Site

### 1. Browse 15 Budget-Friendly Recipes (`Recipes & Rules`)
- **Search & Filter Dishes:** Search by dish name or ingredient, or filter between **Core Repertoire (7)**, **New Pantry Additions (8)**, **Quick Skillets (<25 mins)**, and **Batch & Oven Bakes**.
- **Step-by-Step Cooking Cards:** Click any recipe to open full ingredients from your Checkers basket, clear cooking instructions, storage/freezing tips, and serving ideas.
- **Kitchen Preservation Rules:** Learn simple habits—like stretching 500g of mince with brown lentils and grated carrots into 6–8 portions, prepping day-old rice for crispy fried rice, and keeping a whole cabbage crisp for over 3 weeks.

### 2. Shop Smarter with the Checkers Trolley (`Checkers Trolley`)
- **Organized by Store Flow:** Your 29-item grocery list is sorted in the order you walk through Checkers—**Entrance Produce & Bakery → Centre Grains & Canned Aisles → Butchery & Cold Chain**—so you never have to backtrack.
- **Shop by Trip:** Filter between **All (29)**, **Bulk Haul 1 (~R904)** at the start of the month, and smaller weekly fresh top-ups (**Week 2 ~R185**, **Week 3 ~R244**, **Week 4 ~R143**).
- **In-Store Mode:** Switch on **In-Store Mode** on your phone for larger tap targets, a sticky bottom bar showing your live trolley count and running spend, and automatic sorting that moves checked items neatly to the bottom.
- **Saved Progress & Undo Safety:** Checked items stay saved on your device between trips, and clearing your list gives you a 7-second **Undo** option if you tap it by mistake.
- **Print-Friendly List:** Use **Print List** at any time for a clean, paper-friendly shopping checklist.

### 3. Follow the 4-Week Dinner Plan (`4-Week Calendar`)
- **Week-by-Week Schedule:** Switch across all four weeks—from curries and roast chicken in Week 1, pasta bakes and skillets in Week 2, the Lentil-Stretched Mince Master Week in Week 3, to end-of-month pantry meals in Week 4.
- **Batch Cook + 5-Minute Reheats:** Clearly labels big-batch cooking nights alongside quick 5-minute leftover reheats and 15-minute weeknight skillets.
- **Click to Cook:** Tap any day on the calendar to open that evening's recipe, or swap in any of the 8 extra pantry creations using ingredients you already have.

### 4. Prep the Week in 90 Minutes (`90-Min Prep`)
- **Sunday Parallel Cook Timeline:** A step-by-step 90-minute kitchen guide (`00:00` to `01:30`) that shows how to boil lentils, cook savoury mince, whip mash, chill extra rice, and assemble Wednesday's Cottage Pie in one session—washing pans only once.
