Three Golden Food Prep & Preservation Rules

Practical kitchen habits that stop fresh produce from spoiling, keep weeknight cooking under 20 minutes, and protect your monthly spend.

The Lentil Multiplier

Simmer 1 cup of dry brown lentils separately until tender-firm, then fold them into browned mince with finely grated carrots. They absorb meat juices and double sauce yield with zero dilution of flavour.

The Day-Old Rice Protocol

When boiling rice on Monday, cook 2 extra cups. Spread it flat in a shallow container and chill uncovered for an hour before sealing. Drying surface starch guarantees crisp Friday fried rice instead of mush.

3-Week Cabbage Preservation

Store the whole green cabbage dry and unwashed in the crisper drawer. Never slice the head in half—peel off only the outer leaves you need and shred them. An intact head stays crisp for over three weeks.

The 90-Minute Sunday Parallel Cook

Cook the week's foundations simultaneously on Sunday afternoon. Wash pans once and keep pre-made dinners waiting in the fridge all week.

  1. 00:00 – 00:15 Prep & Par-Boil

    1. Boil Lentils & Shred Roots

    Rinse 1 cup brown lentils and place on the back burner in salted water (18 mins simmer). Meanwhile, finely grate 2 carrots, dice 2 brown onions, and peel 5 potatoes for mash.

  2. 00:15 – 00:45 Active Sauté

    2. Brown Mince & Build Savoury Ragù

    In a large deep pan, brown 500g lean mince in sunflower oil. Remove meat, fry onions and grated carrots. Stir in the drained lentils, canned tomatoes, 2 cups stock, and herbs. Simmer gently for 25 minutes.

  3. 00:45 – 01:05 Carb Foundations

    3. Fluffy Mashed Potatoes & Extra Rice

    Boil potatoes until fork-tender, then mash with 1/4 cup milk and butter. In a second pot, cook 2 extra cups of white rice to chill in the fridge so surface starch dries out for crisp Friday fried rice.

  4. 01:05 – 01:20 Assembly Line

    4. Assemble Cottage Pie & Portion Containers

    Ladle 2 cups of savoury mince into a baking dish, spread mashed potatoes on top, score with a fork, and finish with grated cheddar. Cover with foil for Wednesday's dinner.

  5. 01:20 – 01:30 Kitchen Close

    5. Sink Wash & Fridge Stock

    Wash the two pans. Monday dinner (Bolognese) is ready to serve, Tuesday lunch is packed, Wednesday Cottage Pie is prepped, and Friday's fried rice base is chilled.
