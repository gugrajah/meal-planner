Anchor Culinary Strategy

The Lentil-Stretched Mince Technique

Instead of cooking a tiny portion of mince that vanishes in one evening, combine 500g lean beef or chicken mince with 1 cup of par-cooked brown lentils and 2 finely grated carrots. The lentils absorb the pan drippings and beef stock, expanding into 6–8 full dinner portions from just R62 worth of meat.

Pantry & Food Storage Discipline

Three Golden Food Prep & Preservation Rules

These habits keep fresh produce from spoiling, keep active weeknight cooking under 20 minutes, and protect your food budget.

1

The Lentil Multiplier

When cooking ground mince or stews, simmer 1 cup of dry brown lentils separately until tender-firm. Stir them into browned meat with grated carrots. They drink up meat juices and double sauce yield with zero dilution of flavor.

2

The Day-Old Rice Protocol

When boiling rice on Monday, cook an extra 2 cups. Spread the extra rice flat in a shallow container and chill uncovered for an hour before sealing. This dries surface starch, guaranteeing crisp fried rice on Friday instead of soggy mush.

3

3-Week Cabbage Preservation

Keep the whole green cabbage dry and unwashed in the crisper drawer. When preparing dinner, do not slice the head in half; peel off only the individual outer leaves you need and shred them. An intact head easily stays crisp for over 3 weeks.

Sunday Kitchen Blueprint

The 90-Minute Sunday Parallel Cook

How to cook the week's staples simultaneously on Sunday afternoon. You only wash pans once, and delicious pre-made dinners wait in the fridge all week long.

00:00 – 00:15 Prep & Par-Boil
1. Boil Lentils & Shred Roots

Rinse 1 cup brown lentils and place on back burner in salted water (18 mins simmer). Meanwhile, grate 2 carrots, dice 2 brown onions, and peel 5 potatoes for mash.

00:15 – 00:45 Active Sauté
2. Brown Mince & Build Savoury Ragù

In a large deep pan, brown 500g lean mince in sunflower oil. Remove meat, fry onions and grated carrots. Stir in the drained lentils, canned tomatoes, 2 cups stock, and herbs. Simmer gently for 25 mins.

00:45 – 01:05 Carb Foundations
3. Fluffy Mashed Potatoes & Extra Rice

Boil potatoes until fork-tender, mash with 1/4 cup milk and butter. In a second pot, cook 2 extra cups of white rice to chill in the fridge (dries surface starch for crisp fried rice later).

01:05 – 01:20 Assembly Line
4. Assemble Cottage Pie & Portion Containers

Ladle 2 cups of savoury mince into a baking dish, spread mashed potatoes on top, score with a fork, and top with grated cheddar. Cover with foil for Wednesday's dinner.

01:20 – 01:30 Kitchen Close
5. Sink Wash & Fridge Stock

Wash the two pans. Monday dinner (Bolognese) is ready to eat, Tuesday lunch is packed, Wednesday Cottage Pie is prepped, and Friday fried rice base is chilled!
